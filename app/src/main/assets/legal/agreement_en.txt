ThoughtWeave AI Features Agreement

Version 1. This draft has not been reviewed by a lawyer and is not final.

1. What this agreement covers

This agreement applies to the AI features of ThoughtWeave: summarizing memos and extracting schedules from memos. You can use memos and schedules without accepting it.

2. How the AI features work

The AI features run on your device using the Gemma 3 1B model. Your memos are processed on your device and are not sent to ThoughtWeave or to any AI service. The app does not request permission to access the internet.

The model is about 557 MB and is delivered through Google Play. By default it downloads over Wi-Fi. It downloads over mobile data only if you allow it.

3. AI results can be wrong

AI results may be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date, and the model can make mistakes with dates and with Korean text. Check every result before relying on it. Nothing is saved until you press Save.

Do not rely on AI results for medical, legal, financial or other professional advice, or for decisions where a mistake could cause harm.

Gemma is provided under and subject to the Gemma Terms of Use found at ai.google.dev/gemma/terms

You must follow the Gemma Terms of Use and the Gemma Prohibited Use Policy, both available under Licenses and notices in this app. You must not use the AI features, or allow others to use them, for any use the Gemma Prohibited Use Policy forbids, including:

- generating content that infringes the rights of others;

- performing or facilitating dangerous, illegal or malicious activities;

- generating content intended to deceive, defame, harass or harm others; or

- generating sexually explicit content, except as that policy permits.

You are responsible for how you use AI results, including any content you create from them.

5. No warranty

To the extent permitted by law, the AI features are provided "as is", without warranties of any kind. To the extent permitted by law, ThoughtWeave's provider is not liable for loss or damage arising from AI results. Rights you have under laws that cannot be waived are not affected.

6. Withdrawing your consent

You can withdraw your consent at any time under Licenses and notices. The AI features then stop working until you accept again. Your memos and schedules are not affected.

7. Changes to this agreement

If this agreement changes, the app will ask you to accept the new version before you use the AI features again.
